Changes in specific entropy of ideal gases with constant specific heats
\(\displaystyle s_2-s_1 = s(T_2,v_2)-s(T_1,v_1) = c_v\ln\frac{T_2}{T_1} + R\ln\frac{v_2}{v_1}\)
\(\displaystyle s_2-s_1 = s(T_2,p_2)-s(T_1,p_1) = c_p\ln\frac{T_2}{T_1} - R\ln\frac{p_2}{p_1}\)
\(\displaystyle R = \frac{\bar R}{M}\)
Symbols
  • \(s\) — specific entropy
  • \(T\) — thermodynamic temperature
  • \(v\) — specific volume
  • \(p\) — absolute pressure
  • \(R\) — gas constant
  • \(\bar R\) — universal gas constant, 8314 [J/(kmol·K)]
  • \(M\) — molar mass
  • \(c_v\) — constant-volume specific heat
  • \(c_p\) — constant-pressure specific heat
  • subscript 1 — state 1, subscript 2 — state 2
