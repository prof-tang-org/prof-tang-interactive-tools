\(\displaystyle \frac{T_2}{T_1} = \left(\frac{v_1}{v_2}\right)^{k-1}\)
\(\displaystyle T_2 = T_1\left(\frac{v_1}{v_2}\right)^{k-1} \qquad \text{and} \qquad v_2 = v_1\left(\frac{T_1}{T_2}\right)^{1/(k-1)}\)
Symbols
  • \(T\) — thermodynamic temperature
  • \(v\) — specific volume
  • \(k\) — specific heat ratio, \(c_p / c_v\)
  • subscript \(1\) — state 1
  • subscript \(2\) — state 2
