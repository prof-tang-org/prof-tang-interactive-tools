Equation of state of ideal gases relating pressure, specific volume, and temperature, i.e., ideal gas law
\(\displaystyle pv = RT \quad \text{where} \quad R = \frac{\bar R}{M}\)
Symbols
  • \(p\) — absolute pressure
  • \(v\) — specific volume
  • \(T\) — thermodynamic temperature
  • \(R\) — gas constant
  • \(\bar R\) — universal gas constant, 8314 [J/(kmol·K)]
  • \(M\) — molar mass

Inputs & Outputs
